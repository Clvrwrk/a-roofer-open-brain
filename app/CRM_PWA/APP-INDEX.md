# CRM app home

Owning source: [Clvrwrk/CRM_PWA](https://github.com/Clvrwrk/CRM_PWA), including `apps/crm`, shared `packages`, migrations and release scripts. The local `source` link opens the existing CRM checkout without relocating it or switching its branch.

This home also preserves local PRDs and prototype intake. Curated groupings may use `planning/` and `prototypes/`; raw documents, archives, agent worktrees and source links stay ignored. A product document is not a second executable CRM.

Read the [shared-system boundary](../../docs/111-crm-pwa-companion-repo.md) before changing identity, property data, permissions or Sales routing.
