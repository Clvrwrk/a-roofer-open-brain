### AccuLynx job-link coverage fixed (17:25 CT) — mig 237

**185 → 863 of 1,089 ABC invoices linked (17% → 79%).** Credit-memo surface: **73 → 241
of 261** deep-linkable; job labels 154 → 254.

Two causes, both in the join key of `v_invoice_acculynx_match`:
1. Invoice side normalised the WHOLE label — AccuLynx "KS-79: Mark McCall" → `KS79`, but
   ABC `order_name` "KS-79 Mark McCall" (no colon) → `KS79MARKMCCALL`. Never matched.
2. **The view only ever read `order_name`. The job is usually in
   `purchase_order_number`** ("Customer PO#" on the printed invoice IS the job key:
   `KS-160-1`, `ks158`, `CO-354-1`). That field was never consulted — the bulk of the loss.

Fix = keep exact match as tier 1, add canonical **job token** fallback (leading
`<2 letters><digits>`; the trailing `-1` is the material sequence, not the job).
Tier 2 from order_name (+22), tier 3 from the PO (+656). New `link_method` column.

**Safety proof**: 883 non-temp prefixed jobs → 883 DISTINCT tokens, zero collisions.
Tier-1 fingerprint AFTER the migration (`md5 7508797978d4ae5f9aec66485757c9ca`, 185 rows)
is byte-identical to the pre-migration fingerprint. Nothing that linked before moved.

**Blast radius (stated, not slipped in)**: `executive-pipeline.ts` attributes invoice cost
to jobs via this view on `matched = true`. 185 → 863 MOVES job-cost and margin on the
executive dashboard. Correct, but visible.

**Ceiling is 241, not 261.** The 13 memos with a label but no link are not jobs at all —
